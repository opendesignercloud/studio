How it works

  1. Drop in your logo or a picture. The preview shows it as a browser tab icon and at each size, on light and dark browsers.
  2. Choose how it fits, its shape, padding and background. Fill crops to a square, and Fit keeps the whole image.
  3. Download the ZIP, then upload its files to the top level of your website and paste the HTML into your page head.

What is in the ZIP?

  • favicon.ico with 16, 32 and 48 pixel icons, for browsers and Windows.
  • favicon-16x16.png and favicon-32x32.png for modern browsers.
  • apple-touch-icon.png (180 pixels) for iPhone and iPad home screens.
  • android-chrome-192x192.png and android-chrome-512x512.png for Android and installed web apps.
  • site.webmanifest, which links the Android icons and your theme colour.
  • head-snippet.html, the lines to paste inside the head of your pages.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The icons are made by your own browser and your image never leaves your device. To understand how the tools are used, we keep cookie-free page-view counts and simple usage counts (which tool ran and whether it worked). We never see your images, file names or personal details.

What makes a good favicon?

Icons are tiny, so simple wins. Use a bold shape or a single letter, avoid fine detail and small text, and keep some padding. Check the 16 pixel preview: if you can still recognise it there, it will work in a tab. A transparent background suits most logos, but a solid colour helps a dark logo stay visible on a dark browser.

What are the limits?

Images up to 30 MB. SVG logos are drawn large, so they stay sharp at every size. This tool does not make maskable Android icons, so keep important details away from the edges. iPhone HEIC photos need to be exported as JPG or PNG first.
