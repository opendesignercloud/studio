    How it works

    1. Drop in one image or a whole batch.
    2. Choose the format, quality and maximum size. Results appear straight away and update when you change an option.
    3. Download files one at a time, or all at once as a ZIP.

    Which format should I choose?

    • JPG for photos that need to open anywhere. It gives small files but cannot be transparent.
    • PNG for logos, screenshots and artwork. It is lossless and keeps transparency, but photos make large files.
    • WebP for websites. It is usually smaller than JPG or PNG and keeps transparency.
    • AVIF for the smallest files. It is newer, so some apps cannot open it, and the AVIF button is disabled if your browser cannot create AVIF files.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Your images are converted by your own browser and never leave your device. To understand how the tools are used, we keep cookie-free page-view counts and simple usage counts (which tool ran and whether it worked). We never see your images, file names or personal details.

    What happens to photo details and colour profiles?

    Converting redraws the image, so camera details and location data (EXIF) are removed, and the colour profile becomes standard sRGB.

    What are the limits?

    Up to 40 images at a time, each up to 60 MB. Images larger than 8192 pixels on the longest side are reduced to fit. Animated GIFs are converted from their first frame, and SVG files are drawn at 2048 pixels. iPhone HEIC photos need to be exported as JPG or PNG first.
