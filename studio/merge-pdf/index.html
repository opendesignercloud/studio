Studio / Merge PDF

Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one. Every page shows as a thumbnail, so you can drag pages into the order you want, rotate them, and remove the ones you do not need. It all happens in your browser, so your files are never uploaded.

Getting ready

Your PDFs

    How it works

    1. Drop in two or more PDFs. Their pages appear in the order you added them.
    2. Drag a page to a new spot, or use the arrow buttons under it. Use the rotate button to turn a page, and the cross to remove it. Remove a whole PDF from the panel on the right.
    3. Name the file and choose Merge and download.

    Are my PDFs uploaded?

    No. Your PDFs are read and combined by your own browser and never leave your device. To understand how the tools are used, we keep cookie-free page-view counts and simple usage counts (which tool ran and whether it worked). We never see your files, their contents or their names.

    Will the merged PDF keep its quality?

    Yes. Pages are copied across as they are, not turned into pictures, so text stays sharp and selectable, links keep working and the file size stays close to the sum of the originals. Thumbnails are only a preview.

    What does not carry over?

    The merged file keeps each page's content, but document-level extras from the originals, such as bookmarks, form fields that are shared between pages and the original title and author, are not carried over. Password-protected PDFs are not supported: remove the password first.

    What are the limits?

    Up to 20 PDFs at a time, each up to 100 MB, and 1,000 pages in total. Very large files may be slow on older phones and tablets, because everything runs on your own device.
